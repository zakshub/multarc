# Character 001 — Story 01 Beat Sheet v0.1

**Working Story Title:** The Extra Room  
**Status:** Derived from Narrative Draft v0.1

## Beat 01 — Mundane Assignment
CHR-001 arrives to verify an apartment planning discrepancy that building management considers clerical.

## Beat 02 — Physical Contradiction
The third bedroom exists physically but cannot fit within the approved building geometry.

## Beat 03 — Human Evidence
Sami reveals that records of his presence have begun failing.

## Beat 04 — Method Shift
CHR-001 abandons digital documentation and switches to paper, analog tools, and redundant physical measurement.

## Beat 05 — Midpoint Discovery
The room is not expanding. The rest of the floor is subtly deforming around it.

This changes the problem from hidden construction to active spatial contradiction.

## Beat 06 — Escalation
Sami enters the room to retrieve his phone.

The door closes.

Mariam immediately forgets she has a son.

## Beat 07 — Personal Trigger
CHR-001 recognizes the pattern as emotionally connected to an unresolved event from age twenty three.

He chooses to enter.

## Beat 08 — Impossible Interior
The room has become a corridor of repeating variants of itself.

Sight becomes unreliable.

## Beat 09 — False Rescue
CHR-001 encounters a version of Sami that knows most personal details but fails one mundane memory test.

The anomaly is capable of producing convincing identity copies or alternatives.

## Beat 10 — Acoustic Solution
CHR-001 uses timed knocks, analog measurements, temperature, and sound delay to identify the correct route.

## Beat 11 — Climax
He finds the real Sami and returns through collapsing geometry.

## Beat 12 — Local Resolution
The apartment returns to two bedrooms.

Mariam remembers Sami.

Digital family records mostly restore.

## Beat 13 — Cost
The building's systems no longer contain evidence that CHR-001 was ever present.

## Beat 14 — Long-Term Hook
A symbol from the vanished room matches one recorded in CHR-001's age twenty three incident.

## Beat 15 — Final Unease
A sixth-floor window lights up where no room should exist.
