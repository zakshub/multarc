# PAGE 1 — ARRIVAL AND FIRST CONTRADICTION

## Panel 1
Wide exterior of an aging apartment building in late afternoon.

Caption:
By four in the afternoon, the building had already lied to him three times.

## Panel 2
CHR-001 across the street using a laser measure on the facade.

Farid stands nearby, checking his phone.

Farid:
It is a paperwork problem.

## Panel 3
Close view of CHR-001's notebook.

Visible handwritten figures:
Exterior width  
Floor span  
Reference line

## Panel 4
Inside elevator.

Farid talks.

CHR-001 watches floor numbers and listens to the elevator mechanism instead of making eye contact.

Farid:
Old building. Bad records. Renovations nobody declared.

## Panel 5
Sixth-floor corridor.

CHR-001 measures from elevator to fire stair.

## Panel 6
Reverse direction measurement.

The number differs.

Small note in notebook:
+35 cm

## Panel 7
Door to Apartment 6B opens.

Mariam:
The plan says two bedrooms.

Beat.

Mariam:
Come count them.

# PAGE 3 — SAMI AND FAILING RECORDS

## Panel 1
Sami appears in the hallway with headphones around his neck.

Sami:
How long is this going to take?

## Panel 2
CHR-001:
How long has this room been here?

Sami:
Always.

## Panel 3
Mariam:
We moved here nine years ago. It was here then.

Farid:
No approved plan has ever shown it.

## Panel 4
CHR-001:
Anything else wrong lately?

Sami:
School marked me absent yesterday.

## Panel 5
Sami:
My friend had a photo from class. I was in it.

Beat.

Sami:
Then I wasn't.

## Panel 6
Digital family frame.

Image shows Mariam, Sami, younger daughter.

Glitch.

Sami disappears for half a second.

## Panel 7
CHR-001 closes his tablet case and opens paper notebook.

CHR-001:
From now on, paper.

# PAGE 9 — RETURN

## Panel 1
CHR-001 and Sami following survey cord.

## Panel 2
Cord reaches a junction and splits into three identical lines.

Sami:
Which one?

## Panel 3
CHR-001 tests each line with steel ruler and fingertips.

Two lines warm.

One cold.

## Panel 4
Farid's knock vibrates faintly through the cold line.

CHR-001:
That one.

## Panel 5
Corridor stretches unnaturally.

Walls narrow.

Floor tilts.

They run.

## Panel 6
Exit door appears.

Mariam visible beyond it.

## Panel 7
Sami crosses threshold.

Mariam sees him.

Recognition hits instantly.

Mariam:
SAMI!

Behind them, corridor folds into darkness.
