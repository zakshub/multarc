## Scene 01 — Exterior and Sixth Floor Arrival

**Location:** Aging apartment building, exterior and sixth-floor corridor  
**Characters:** CHR-001, Farid  
**Objective:** Establish professional context and CHR-001's observational method  
**Conflict:** Building management wants a simple clerical explanation  
**Change:** The floor measurements already contradict themselves  
**Exit Beat:** Apartment 6B contains a third bedroom

## Scene 02 — The Third Bedroom

**Location:** Apartment 6B  
**Characters:** CHR-001, Farid, Mariam  
**Objective:** Verify whether the room is undocumented construction  
**Conflict:** The room measures correctly but cannot physically fit inside the building  
**Change:** The impossible window proves the problem is geometric, not administrative  
**Exit Beat:** Sami enters the story

## Scene 03 — Sami and the Record Failures

**Location:** Apartment 6B  
**Characters:** CHR-001, Mariam, Sami, Farid  
**Objective:** Determine whether the anomaly has affected people  
**Conflict:** Sami reports attendance and photo inconsistencies  
**Change:** CHR-001 witnesses Sami disappear momentarily from a digital family image  
**Exit Beat:** CHR-001 abandons digital tools for paper

## Scene 04 — The Building Makes Space

**Location:** 6B, 6C reference wall, sixth-floor corridor  
**Characters:** CHR-001, Farid, Mariam, Sami  
**Objective:** Map the contradiction  
**Conflict:** Repeated measurements continue changing  
**Change:** CHR-001 realizes the room is stable while surrounding architecture deforms around it  
**Exit Beat:** He marks the room as an anchor and warns everyone not to enter

## Scene 05 — Sami Disappears

**Location:** Apartment 6B  
**Characters:** CHR-001, Sami, Mariam, Farid  
**Objective:** Keep everyone outside the anomaly  
**Conflict:** Sami's phone rings from inside and he enters to retrieve it  
**Change:** Door closes and Mariam instantly forgets she has a son  
**Exit Beat:** CHR-001 decides to enter

## Scene 06 — Preparation at the Threshold

**Location:** Hallway outside former third bedroom  
**Characters:** CHR-001, Farid, Mariam  
**Objective:** Establish an analog return path  
**Conflict:** Farid wants the room sealed  
**Change:** CHR-001 anchors survey cord and assigns timed knocks  
**Exit Beat:** Door opens onto impossible space

## Scene 07 — Residual Corridor

**Location:** Repeating-bedroom corridor  
**Characters:** CHR-001  
**Objective:** Find Sami and preserve route information  
**Conflict:** Visual geometry duplicates and invalidates chalk markers  
**Change:** CHR-001 stops trusting sight and begins mapping by sound  
**Exit Beat:** He hears a response that is not Farid's

## Scene 08 — False Sami

**Location:** Variant bedroom  
**Characters:** CHR-001, false or alternate Sami  
**Objective:** Verify identity before extraction  
**Conflict:** The boy appears correct and knows personal information

**Exit Beat:** All corridor doors open simultaneously

## Scene 09 — Real Sami and Return Route

**Location:** Deeper residual corridor  
**Characters:** CHR-001, Sami  
**Objective:** Find and extract the real Sami  
**Conflict:** Multiple return cords appear and the corridor continues shifting  
**Change:** CHR-001 combines acoustics, physical temperature, and vibration to choose the correct line  
**Exit Beat:** The exit door appears

## Scene 10 — Restoration

**Location:** Apartment 6B  
**Characters:** CHR-001, Sami, Mariam, Farid, Mariam's daughter  
**Objective:** Confirm whether local reality has stabilized

## Scene 11 — Referential Cost

**Location:** Street outside building  
**Characters:** CHR-001  
**Objective:** Verify his own record of participation  
**Conflict:** Visitor logs and surveillance no longer show him  
**Change:** Analog notebook remains intact

## Scene 12 — Final Image

**Location:** Street / building exterior  
**Characters:** CHR-001  
**Objective:** End on unresolved pressure rather than explanation  
**Conflict:** Reality may still be actively destabilizing his presence
