# Character 001 — Identity Bible v0.2

**Character ID:** CHR-001  
**Canonical Name:** Not assigned  
**Primary Launch Age:** Approximately 29–30  
**Reference Progression Range:** Approximately 23–40  
**Status:** Development

## Identity Principle

Character 001 must remain recognizably the same individual throughout approximately seventeen years of visible aging.

The system must preserve genetic identity while allowing life history to alter grooming, posture, body composition, wardrobe, fatigue, scars, and emotional wear.

## Stable Identity Anchors

### Head / Face
- elongated oval facial foundation
- relatively lean facial proportions
- moderately defined jaw rather than an extremely square jaw
- balanced cheek structure

### Eyes
- large, highly expressive eyes
- strong eye presence
- slightly deep-set visual impression
- gaze is a primary recognizability anchor

### Eyebrows
- naturally thick
- prominent
- strong horizontal visual weight

### Nose
- relatively long central structure
- defined bridge
- consistent projection across stylization and aging

### Mouth
- restrained natural mouth width
- capable of a characteristic asymmetric or knowing half-smile

### Hair
- naturally dark
- dense
- substantial volume
- capable of loose youthful styling, controlled medium styling, short professional styling, and longer mature styling

### Facial Hair
Allowed progression:
- light stubble
- short shaped beard
- medium full beard
- heavy mature beard

The beard may evolve, but the underlying jaw and mouth proportions must remain fixed.

## Body Identity

- lean to medium build
- not naturally bodybuilder-heavy
- relatively narrow waist
- proportionate shoulders
- body mass may change, skeletal frame should not

## Age Stages

## Permanent Identity Uniform — Mature Character System

The supplied age-40 references establish a permanent identity uniform that should remain recognizable across mature appearances unless the story explicitly requires disguise, formal dress, injury treatment, or environment-specific gear.

### Core Uniform Components
- dark charcoal or washed-black plain T-shirt
- earthy brown / tobacco / dark khaki cargo-jogger trousers
- practical brown low-profile casual shoes / rugged sneakers
- large rugged square mechanical-style wristwatch with exposed technical detailing, dark strap, brushed metal case, and industrial yellow/gold accent hardware
- minimal dark wrist band / bracelet when appropriate
- optional simple dog-tag style pendant or narrow metal pendant
- long dark hair with natural grey progression, usually pushed back, loose, or tied depending on activity
- full mature beard with natural grey progression

### Identity Function
This uniform is not costume decoration. It is part of the character's silhouette and recognizability system.

The intended read is:
- utilitarian
- non-fashion-dependent
- intelligent but not polished
- rugged
- physically practical
- visually stable across scenes
- credible in thriller, horror, science fiction, conspiracy, and grounded dramatic contexts

### Hard Continuity Rule
Do not randomly replace the mature uniform with generic jackets, jeans, formalwear, superhero suits, or fashion-forward styling unless the narrative explicitly motivates the change.
