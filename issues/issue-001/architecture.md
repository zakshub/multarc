# Issue 001 — Launch Architecture v0.1

**Series:** MULTARC  
**Issue:** 001  
**Format:** Anthology launch issue  
**Status:** Development  
**Target Length:** 32 pages including cover and editorial transitions

## Editorial Purpose

Issue 001 must prove that MULTARC can support multiple independent characters and genres inside one shared continuity without requiring an exposition-heavy universe introduction.

The issue should feel like the beginning of a large world, not a compressed encyclopedia.

## Structure

### Cover — 1 page
A unified anthology cover that does not privilege a future team concept. The visual promise is mystery, scale, and tonal range.

### Feature A — Character 001 / THE SURVEYOR — 10 pages
Primary genre: liminal science-fiction mystery / horror-thriller.

Purpose:
- establish the first recurring protagonist
- demonstrate the franchise rule that impossible phenomena have systems and costs
- introduce Residual Architecture
- end with a local resolution and a larger unanswered implication

### Feature B — Character 002 — 8 pages
Primary genre intentionally different from Feature A.

Purpose:
- prove genre range
- introduce a second standalone narrative node
- avoid any direct crossover in Issue 001

### Feature C — Character 003 — 8 pages
Primary genre intentionally different again.

Purpose:
- push conceptual range
- introduce a third independent protagonist or anti-protagonist

### Experimental Short — 3 pages
A compact one-shot, artifact story, field report, horror fragment, visual puzzle, or continuity seed.

Purpose:
- create anthology texture
- provide a format for ideas that do not yet justify full recurring characters

### Editorial / Transition Material — 2 pages
Used for title pages, chapter breaks, index/credits, or controlled continuity hints.

## Panel Density

Default target: 6–9 panels per story page.

Exceptions:
- splash or reveal: 1–3 panels
- action compression: 7–10 panels
- dialogue / investigative rhythm: 5–8 panels
- horror pacing: variable, with negative space used deliberately

Panel count is not a quota. Every panel must advance information, character, atmosphere, action, or consequence.

## Launch Rules

1. No team formation.
2. No multiverse exposition dump.
3. No crossover between the main features.
4. No cosmic hierarchy lecture.
5. Each feature must work independently.
6. Each feature must contain one memorable visual concept.
7. Each feature must leave one unresolved pressure point.
8. No story exists only to tease another story.
9. Background details may establish continuity, but they must not be required to understand the story.
10. Comic-first pacing; later animation adaptation remains possible.

## Issue-Level Continuity Strategy

Issue 001 may include one subtle recurring symbol, organization trace, object, phrase, architectural mark, or data signature across multiple features.

This must function as optional discovery, not required exposition.

## Current Production Order

1. Lock Feature A story architecture.
2. Produce Feature A page-by-page script.
3. Build Character 001 visual consistency assets.
4. Generate first production artifact.
5. Continue Character 002 and Character 003 development.
