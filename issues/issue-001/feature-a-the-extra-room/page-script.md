# Issue 001 — Feature A Page Script v0.1

**Story:** THE EXTRA ROOM  
**Length:** 10 pages  
**Character:** CHR-001

---

## PAGE 1 — THE JOB
**Target panels:** 7

1. Exterior establishing shot: aging mid-rise apartment building in late afternoon.
2. CHR-001 standing across the street, measuring facade width with a laser tool.
3. Close-up of his notebook: exterior width written down.
4. Building manager beside him, impatient, saying the complaint is "just a paperwork mismatch."
5. Elevator interior. CHR-001 watches floor numbers instead of making conversation.
6. Hallway outside 6B. The corridor appears ordinary.
7. Door opens. Tenant says: "The plan says two bedrooms. Come count them."

**Page turn question:** What is wrong with the apartment?

---

## PAGE 2 — THE EXTRA ROOM
**Target panels:** 8

1. Quick visual count: bedroom one.
2. Bedroom two.
3. Hallway turn.
4. Third door.
5. CHR-001 stops before entering.
6. Interior of third room: completely ordinary.
7. He checks the approved plan on tablet: no room.
8. He looks toward the room's window.

---

## PAGE 3 — THE IMPOSSIBLE WINDOW
**Target panels:** 6

1. View through the window: rear alley.
2. CHR-001 looks at compass / orientation.
3. Exterior diagram in notebook: this wall should face another apartment, not the alley.
4. He measures room length.
5. He repeats measurement with analog tape.
6. Both measurements match.

**Key line:** "The instruments agree. The building doesn't."

---

## PAGE 4 — THE BOY
**Target panels:** 7

1. Tenant's son appears in doorway, annoyed by the inspection.
2. Mother explains he has slept there for months.
3. CHR-001 asks whether anything has gone missing.
4. Mother says no.
5. Son says: "My school says I was absent yesterday. I was there."
6. CHR-001 notices family photo on shelf failing to load on a digital frame.
7. He quietly switches from tablet to paper notebook.

---

## PAGE 5 — THE BUILDING SHIFTS
**Target panels:** 8

1. CHR-001 measures apartment width.
2. Measures hallway.
3. Measures neighboring unit reference wall.
4. Writes figures.
5. Repeats.
6. New figures differ by centimeters.
7. Overlay diagram showing floor plan subtly deforming around Room 3.
8. CHR-001 realizes: "It's not inside the building. The building is moving around it."

---

## PAGE 6 — LOSS OF REFERENCE
**Target panels:** 7

1. Building manager checks visitor system: no record of CHR-001 signing in.
2. CHR-001 photographs the room.
3. Photo metadata shows impossible timestamp.
4. Mother calls school; they say she has one child, not two.
5. Son gets angry and enters the room to retrieve his phone.
6. Door closes.
7. Mother turns to CHR-001: "Who are you waiting for?"

**Page turn shock:** The son has been forgotten.

---

## PAGE 7 — THE CHOICE
**Target panels:** 6

1. CHR-001 stares at the closed door.
2. His hand tightens around old paper notebook.
3. Brief insert: an old page with an unfinished diagram, no context.
4. Building manager says they should seal the room.
5. CHR-001 ties a physical line to the hallway radiator.
6. He opens the door and steps through.

---

## PAGE 8 — RESIDUAL CORRIDOR
**Target panels:** 5

1. Large reveal: corridor of repeating versions of the bedroom.
2. Each room contains subtle variations: different posters, bed positions, lighting, objects.
3. CHR-001 marks wall with chalk.
4. Chalk mark appears on several doors at once.
5. He stops trusting sight and presses ear to wall.

Atmosphere over exposition.

---

## PAGE 9 — ACOUSTIC MAP
**Target panels:** 8

1. CHR-001 taps wall.
2. Cut to manager outside hearing a faint knock.
3. CHR-001 times echo.
4. Repeats from another threshold.
5. Notebook diagram: sound delay relationships.
6. He identifies one doorway.
7. Opens it: boy sitting on bed, confused.
8. CHR-001 grabs him and follows the physical line back.

---

## PAGE 10 — AFTER
**Target panels:** 7

1. Normal apartment. Only two bedrooms.
2. Mother holds son tightly.
3. Digital family photo restores.
4. CHR-001 checks visitor log: his name is absent.
5. Outside building at night, he writes the case in paper notebook.
6. He turns to an old page: same strange symbol as one found behind the room's skirting.
7. Close-up of his face, then notebook closing.

**End caption:** None.

The story ends on evidence, not explanation.
