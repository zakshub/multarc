# CHR-001 — Wardrobe & Watch Detail Sheet Specification v1.0

**Character ID:** CHR-001  
**Name:** Not assigned  
**Primary Launch Age:** 29–30  
**Purpose:** Lock the permanent utilitarian identity system for repeatable comic and animation use.

## Scope

This sheet focuses only on:
- T-shirt
- trousers
- shoes
- rugged square watch
- optional wristband
- optional small pendant if later approved

No environment design.
No title artwork.
No age progression.
No action scene.
No character renaming.

## Launch Uniform

### T-Shirt
- washed charcoal / near-black
- plain
- crew neck
- relaxed practical fit
- no visible brand
- no graphic print
- subtle fabric wear permitted

### Trousers
- earthy brown / dark khaki
- cargo-jogger influence
- practical pockets
- tapered lower leg
- not military costume
- not fashion-forward

### Shoes
- practical brown low-profile casual shoes / rugged sneakers
- worn-in but maintained
- no bright branding
- no tactical boot exaggeration

### Watch
This is the strongest wearable identity anchor.

Requirements:
- large rugged square case
- brushed metal / titanium-like finish
- dark strap
- visible mechanical or technical complexity
- industrial yellow / muted gold accent detail
- premium but functional
- substantial wrist presence
- should remain recognizable in silhouette even when simplified for animation

The watch should not be an exact copy of a real branded watch.

## Continuity

At age 29–30:
- uniform appears newer
- watch is already present if chronology permits

At age 38–40:
- same core uniform language
- more wear
- darker, rougher, more settled
- mature beard and hair evolution
- watch remains a continuity anchor
